## TONE SKILL: Professional & Corporate

TYPOGRAPHY: Playfair Display for H1/H2. Inter for everything else. Medium weights.
SPACING: Standard 80px sections.
COLORS: Navy #0f172a + one accent (user primaryColor or #00a86b). Cream for light sections.
LANGUAGE: Confident, authoritative. Facts and credentials. Trust signals prominent.
STRUCTURE: Always include stats and testimonials sections. Social proof is critical.
BUTTONS: Standard size. Clean gradient.
DO NOT: Use playful language. Use too many colors. Skip testimonials/trust sections.
