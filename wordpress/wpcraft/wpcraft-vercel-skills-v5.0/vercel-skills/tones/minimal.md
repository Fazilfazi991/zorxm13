## TONE SKILL: Minimal & Clean

TYPOGRAPHY: Inter for everything. Light weights (300-400) for body. No serifs except H1.
SPACING: Ultra generous. 100px+ padding. Lots of whitespace.
COLORS: White #ffffff dominant. Single accent color. No gradients.
LANGUAGE: Simple, direct. Short paragraphs. No buzzwords.
CARDS: No box shadow. Just subtle border #e5e7eb.
BUTTONS: Small-medium size. Borderless or minimal outline.
DO NOT: Use gradient backgrounds. Use multiple accent colors. Use heavy typography.
