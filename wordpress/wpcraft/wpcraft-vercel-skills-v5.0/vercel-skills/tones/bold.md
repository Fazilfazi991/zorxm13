## TONE SKILL: Bold & Energetic

TYPOGRAPHY: Heavy weights (800-900). Large hero text (64px+). Tight letter-spacing.
SPACING: Tight, dynamic. Sections 60-80px padding.
COLORS: High contrast. Dark + bright accent. Green #00a86b or user primaryColor as dominant accent.
LANGUAGE: Direct, punchy. Short sentences. Strong verbs. Numbers. Results-first.
BUTTONS: Full-width or extra-large. Strong shadows.
HERO: Always 2-col with full-height visual impact.
DO NOT: Use serif fonts for body. Use gentle language. Use cream backgrounds.
