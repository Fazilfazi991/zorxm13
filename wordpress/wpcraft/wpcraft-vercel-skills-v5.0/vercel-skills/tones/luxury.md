## TONE SKILL: Luxury & Elegant

TYPOGRAPHY: Playfair Display for ALL headings. Never Poppins as primary. Generous line-height (1.2+).
SPACING: Extra padding — sections at 100px+ top/bottom. Breathing room everywhere.
COLORS: Navy #0f172a dominates. Gold #c9a961 for all accents, CTAs, highlights. Cream #f8f7f3 for light sections.
LANGUAGE: "Crafted" "Curated" "Exclusive" "Bespoke" "Premier" "Distinguished" — never casual, never exclamation marks.
BUTTONS: Rounded (borderRadius:8). Gold gradient only. Large size.
CARDS: Clean, minimal. White with subtle border. Never bright colors.
IMAGES: High-end Unsplash photos. Architectural, aspirational, cinematic.
DO NOT: Use bright green as primary. Use playful language. Use emoji. Use bright backgrounds.
